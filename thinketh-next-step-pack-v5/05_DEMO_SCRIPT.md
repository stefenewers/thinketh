# Thinketh Demo Script

## Opening

"AI knows more every day. Humans don't have a system maintaining what they know."

Open Today.

"Thinketh compares the changing world against a model of what I already understand."

Point to:

"You missed 6 things worth knowing."

"Three are major. Eleven minutes."

Tap filtered count.

"It looked at far more than this. It filtered 143 items because they didn't change my understanding."

## Development

Open lead development.

"Most AI products would summarize this article. Thinketh starts with my current mental model."

Point to:

- You already knew
- What changed
- Updated mental model

"So it doesn't reteach me what I already know."

## Diagnostic

Tap Check My Understanding.

Answer.

When Knowledge Update animation appears:

"This is the important part. Thinketh doesn't just remember that I read something. It updates a computational model of what I understand based on evidence."

Open Mind.

"You can literally see the model change."

## Real-resource kill shot

Open Library.

"That daily brief is not a closed demo. I can give Thinketh something it has never seen before."

Tap Add Resource.

Paste a known-good real URL.

While processing:

"It reads the source, maps the concepts, and compares them against my Mind."

Show the result.

"Full read: 18 minutes."

"But only six minutes are actually useful for me."

Point to:

- You already understand
- New to you
- Connects to your Mind

"That is the knowledge delta."

Tap Teach Me the Delta.

"Instead of summarizing the entire piece, it teaches only what changes my model."

Optionally continue to diagnostic.

## Ask

Open Ask if time.

"Ask Thinketh is also grounded in the same user model and source corpus."

Show Quick / Teach Me / Go Deep briefly.

## Voice

Open Catch Me Up.

"And when I don't want to read any of it..."

Start voice.

Let ElevenLabs speak.

Ask a short follow-up or interrupt once.

## Architecture close

"Mongo models the changing world."

"Tiger models my changing understanding over time."

"Backboard remembers qualitative things about me that don't belong in a numeric knowledge score."

"Claude reasons over the delta."

"ElevenLabs turns that delta into a conversation."

"Thinketh decides what I need next."

## Final line

"Thinketh isn't summarizing the internet. It's maintaining a continuously updated computational model of what the world knows and what I know, then calculating the difference."
